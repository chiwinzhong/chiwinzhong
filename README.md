## What I am building

### AI-native organizations

A framework for distilling experience into structured knowledge, decision rules, workflows, permissions, evaluations, and agents—so an organization can execute and improve without depending on constant human explanation.

### Open-source AI tools

Current experiments include:

* **AI Input Method** — turns scattered human expression into structured, AI-ready intent and measures token savings
* **Understanding Alignment Engine** — adapts the same idea for people with different knowledge, context, and interests
* **KOL MATCH** — an agentic system for matching brand objectives and budgets with suitable creators
* **AI Brand Systems** — turns positioning, content, design rules, and marketing workflows into reusable skills
* **TK-Bank** — one API for accessing Chinese AI models: [tkbank.ai](https://tkbank.ai/)

These are developed from real operating problems rather than demo-first experiments.

## My working principles

1. **Start with the real decision, not the prompt.**
2. **Distill judgment, not just documents.**
3. **Give AI explicit authority, constraints, and evaluation criteria.**
4. **Keep humans responsible for direction, exceptions, and irreversible decisions.**
5. **Treat every completed project as new organizational memory.**
